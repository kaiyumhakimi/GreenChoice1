🌱 GreenChoice Eco Awareness Center

EPEAT

What is EPEAT?

EPEAT is a global ecolabel that evaluates electronic products based on environmental performance, sustainability, and recyclability. Products with Gold certification meet the highest standards.

ENERGY STAR

Why ENERGY STAR Matters

ENERGY STAR certified devices use less electricity, helping consumers save money while reducing carbon emissions.

E-Waste

Proper E-Waste Disposal

Electronic waste contains materials that can harm the environment. Always recycle old electronics at certified collection centers.

Greenwashing

Avoid Greenwashing

Some products claim to be eco-friendly without certification. Always verify environmental claims using trusted labels such as EPEAT and ENERGY STAR.

Energy Saving

Reduce Electricity Usage

Enable power-saving mode, reduce screen brightness, and unplug unused devices to lower electricity consumption.

Sustainability

Extend Product Lifespan

Repair, upgrade, and maintain electronics whenever possible. Extending product lifespan reduces environmental impact.

💡 Daily Eco Tip

Choosing an EPEAT Gold and ENERGY STAR certified device can significantly reduce energy consumption and environmental impact throughout its lifecycle.
